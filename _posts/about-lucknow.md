### Bringing Lucknow's civic data together

There are a lot of initiatives and datasets scattered around, and when you
actually need something it's hard to find. This site puts what's available
for Lucknow in one place: who represents each assembly constituency, the
metro network, neighbourhood amenities, and a community feed for reporting
local issues.

This is a young, incomplete project. It started as a fork of a similar site
built for Bengaluru, and going city to city means going feature by feature —
carrying over what's genuinely portable, and being upfront about what
doesn't have a Lucknow data source yet rather than guessing. See the
[officials directory](/officials) for who represents Lucknow, or
[open the map](/map) to see what's there today.
